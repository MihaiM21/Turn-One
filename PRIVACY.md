# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 28, 2026**

## Overview

Turn One ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Formula 1 data platform.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information

When you register for Turn One, we collect:
- **Account Information**: Email address, username, password (hashed)
- **Profile Information**: Display name, avatar (optional)
- **Contact Information**: Email for account recovery and notifications

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, features used, time spent
- **Device Information**: Browser type, operating system, IP address
- **Technical Data**: API calls, performance metrics, error logs
- **Cookies**: Authentication tokens, preferences, analytics

### F1 Data

- **Race Data**: Telemetry, timing, and race information (publicly available data)
- **User Preferences**: Favorite drivers, teams, notification settings
- **Custom Dashboards**: Your personalized dashboard configurations

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

1. **Provide Services**: Deliver Turn One platform functionality
2. **Authentication**: Secure account access and session management
3. **Personalization**: Customize your experience and preferences
4. **Communication**: Send important updates, security alerts
5. **Analytics**: Improve performance and user experience
6. **Security**: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
7. **Compliance**: Meet legal obligations and enforce terms

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We DO NOT:
-  Sell your personal information to third parties
-  Share data with advertisers for targeted ads
-  Use your data for purposes beyond what's stated here

### We MAY Share Data:
-  **Service Providers**: Hosting, analytics, email services (under strict agreements)
-  **Legal Compliance**: When required by law or to protect rights
-  **Business Transfer**: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale
-  **With Your Consent**: When you explicitly agree

### Third-Party Services

Turn One uses:
- **Hosting**: Cloud infrastructure providers
- **Analytics**: Usage and performance monitoring (anonymized)
- **Email**: Transactional email service providers
- **CDN**: Content delivery for performance

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

- **Encryption in Transit**: TLS 1.3 (HTTPS)
- **Encryption at Rest**: Database encryption available
- **Password Security**: Bcrypt hashing with salt
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access, least privilege principle
- **Audit Logging**: Complete activity tracking
- **Regular Security Scans**: Automated vulnerability scanning
- **Secure Infrastructure**: Firewall, intrusion detection

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts**: Data retained while account is active
- **Inactive Accounts**: Deleted after 2 years of inactivity (with notice)
- **Logs**: Retained for 90 days for security and debugging
- **Audit Trail**: Retained for 7 years for compliance
- **Backups**: Retained for 30 days, then securely deleted

## Your Rights

### GDPR Rights (EU Users)

You have the right to:
1. **Access**: Request a copy of your data
2. **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate data
3. **Erasure**: Delete your account and data ("right to be forgotten")
4. **Restrict Processing**: Limit how we use your data
5. **Data Portability**: Export your data in a standard format
6. **Object**: Opt-out of certain data processing
7. **Withdraw Consent**: At any time

### CCPA Rights (California Users)

You have the right to:
1. **Know**: What personal information is collected
2. **Access**: Request your personal information
3. **Delete**: Request deletion of your information
4. **Opt-Out**: Opt-out of data sale (we don't sell data)
5. **Non-Discrimination**: Equal service regardless of privacy choices

### How to Exercise Your Rights

Contact us at: **privacy@turnone.com**

We will respond within:
- GDPR requests: 30 days
- CCPA requests: 45 days
- General requests: 10 business days
